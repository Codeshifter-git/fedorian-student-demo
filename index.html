How to use this planner

Start on Today. The planner opens on today — one focus task, today's classes, and what's due. That's the whole plan.

Done beats perfect. Mark things done when they are done-ish. A half-finished assignment you handed in counts.

Overdue is not a failure. Late work just sits there patiently until you pick it back up. Nothing turns red, nothing scolds you.

Study in small blocks. Log whatever time you actually studied — ten honest minutes beat a perfect hour you never started.

Everything saves itself. Type, tap, close the tab. It's all here when you come back.
